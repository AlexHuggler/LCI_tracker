Pool Service Software for Sole Traders in Australia

PoolFlow gives an owner-operator the poolside tools that matter—without pretending a solo route needs the same system as a retail shop or dispatch team.

View PoolFlow for iPhone

The software job is to shorten the day, not add admin

For an Australian sole trader, every extra tap competes with testing water, cleaning the pool, speaking with the client and getting to the next suburb. The right tool should capture the visit once and reuse that record for history, inventory cost and route profitability.

PoolFlow centres the work on the pool. Each record can hold the customer, service address, pool volume in litres, equipment, recurring service details and service history. At the visit, the technician records water readings, considers dosing guidance, logs the chemicals actually used and saves notes or a proof-of-service photo. The route dashboard then has better information for the next decision.

A useful buying test

Ask whether a feature helps you complete a visit, protect water quality, reduce driving or understand an account. If it does none of those—and you have no office staff—it may be overhead rather than value.

A day in the PoolFlow workflow

BEFORE THE UTE MOVES

Order the route

Choose today’s stops and optimise the order. Adjust for fixed appointments, school traffic, access windows and the supplier run.

AT THE WATERLINE

Test and dose

Enter the current readings, review LSI and metric dose guidance, then apply professional judgement and the product label.

BEFORE LEAVING

Close the visit

Record the dose actually used, completed work, notes and photo so the next visit begins with context rather than memory.

AT REVIEW TIME

Check contribution

Compare the service fee and chemical cost by pool to find accounts that warrant investigation or repricing.

What is included

  • Pool-specific records: water readings, equipment, visits, notes and photos.
  • Field calculations: LSI and chemical dosing linked to the pool’s actual volume.
  • Australian units: litres, millilitres, kilograms, grams, Celsius and kilometres where relevant.
  • Route support: stop ordering for the day’s selected pools.
  • Cost visibility: inventory unit cost, actual chemical usage and per-pool gross contribution.
  • Offline-first use: core data remains available on the device, with optional iCloud synchronisation.

What it is not

  • It is not a point-of-sale system for a pool shop.
  • It is not an invoicing, payment collection or BAS platform.
  • It is not a customer self-service portal.
  • It is not a multi-technician dispatch console with staff permissions.
  • It is not an Android app.
  • It does not replace product labels, workplace procedures, professional training or judgement.

Choose for the business you have

PoolFlow is a strong fit when the owner is also the technician, the iPhone is the field device and pool chemistry matters more than office automation. It is especially relevant when mobile reception is inconsistent and when chemical spend varies materially across the route.

A larger system is the sensible choice when quoting, invoicing, card payments, accounting integrations, a customer portal, retail POS, formal compliance or staff dispatch must live in the same platform. See the Australian comparison for options with those broader capabilities.
