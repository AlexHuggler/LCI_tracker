How Pool Volume Is Calculated

Pool volume is the foundation of every chemical calculation. Get it wrong and every dose downstream is wrong by the same percentage. Each pool shape uses surface dimensions, an average depth, and a constant that converts cubic feet to gallons:

  • Rectangular: Length × Width × Average Depth × 7.48
  • Round: Diameter × Diameter × Average Depth × 5.9
  • Oval: Long Diameter × Short Diameter × Average Depth × 5.9
  • Freeform / kidney: Length × Width × Average Depth × 7.48 × Shape Factor (0.45–0.55)

Average depth = (Shallow end + Deep end) ÷ 2

For example, a 30 × 15 ft pool with depths of 3.5 ft and 8 ft has an average depth of 5.75 ft, so its volume is 30 × 15 × 5.75 × 7.48 ≈ 19,355 gallons (round to 19,500).
