What PoolFlow Offers That Skimmer Doesn't

These four capabilities are why pool technicians switch from Skimmer to PoolFlow.

Money-Loser Pool Detection

PoolFlow automatically flags every pool where chemical costs eat past 30% of your service fee. See which pools to reprice, reroute, or drop. Most solo techs have 2-3 pools silently draining $100-200/mo in margin. Skimmer has no equivalent feature.

Worth: $100-200/mo in margin you'd never see

LSI-Linked Dosing From Your Costs

Real-time Langelier Saturation Index with CYA-corrected alkalinity. Dosing recommendations are calculated from your actual chemical inventory — your muriatic acid, your soda ash, your cost per ounce. Skimmer offers no chemistry tools.

Worth: ~$50/mo in precise dosing savings

Offline-First Architecture

PoolFlow stores all data locally on your iPhone via Apple's SwiftData framework. Log services, check readings, and optimize routes in dead zones, gated communities, and basements. Skimmer requires an active internet connection to function.

Works in backyards, rural stops, and zero-signal zones

Flat Pricing — No Per-Pool Fees

$29.99/mo for unlimited pools. No per-pool fees, no per-tech fees, no add-on charges. Skimmer's $2/pool/mo means your software bill grows every time you add a customer. At 60 pools, Skimmer costs $218/mo — over 7x what PoolFlow charges.

Worth: $2,256/yr saved at 60 pools
