When PoolFlow may fit

PoolFlow is built for an iPhone-based pool route that benefits from offline service logging, LSI and dosing, route optimization, inventory, and per-pool margin in one app. Pro is a flat in-app subscription rather than a per-location or per-user charge.

When another product may fit

Choose based on the workflow you actually need. Skimmer, Pool Brain, and Pool Office Manager document broader office and billing tools; Jobber spans many home-service trades; PoolTrac uses visited-location pricing; other platforms package users and regional availability differently.

How this comparison is maintained

  4. Prices are presented as published models rather than invented route-size estimates.
