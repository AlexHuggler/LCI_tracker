Why Pool-Specific Software Matters

General field service apps like Jobber are built for HVAC technicians, plumbers, and cleaning crews. They handle scheduling, invoicing, and dispatch well — but they know nothing about pool water.

Pool service is a chemistry-driven business. You need to know your Langelier Saturation Index, dose chemicals based on actual readings and costs, and track which pools are profitable after accounting for chemical spend. No general FSM tool does this.

"Most solo techs have 2-3 pools where chemical costs eat past 30% of the service fee — that's $100-200/mo in margin, gone. A general scheduling app will never flag that. A pool-specific app will."

What general FSM tools are missing

  • No LSI calculator — can't assess water balance at the pool
  • No chemical dosing — you're guessing or doing math by hand
  • No per-pool profitability — you can't see which pools lose money
  • No offline mode — apps fail at poolside without cell signal

What PoolFlow adds for pool techs

  • Real-time LSI calculator with CYA correction
  • Chemical dosing calculated from your actual inventory costs
  • Per-pool profit analytics with automatic money-loser flagging
  • Offline-first — works in dead zones, basements, gated yards
